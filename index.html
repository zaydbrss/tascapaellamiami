A little corner of Spain on Biscayne Bay.

Tasca Paella brings the easygoing tapas bars of Spain to the waterfront at Bayside Marketplace.

Every paella is cooked to order in a wide steel pan until the rice at the bottom turns golden and crisp, the part Spaniards call the socarrat. Around it, the table fills up with croquetas, garlic shrimp and pitchers of sangria, while the boats come and go in the marina.

We call ourselves a fusion kitchen because Miami finds its way into the food: a squeeze of lime in the ceviche, chimichurri on the churrasco, mojo on the grill.
